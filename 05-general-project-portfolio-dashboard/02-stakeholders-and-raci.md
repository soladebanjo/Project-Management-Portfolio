# Stakeholder Register and RACI – Project Portfolio Dashboard

> **Simulated project.** Organisation, people and data are fictional and based on a PM Project Lab practice brief. The PM artefacts are my own work, produced to practise real project-management delivery.

## Stakeholder register
| Stakeholder | Role | Primary concern | Influence | Interest | Engagement approach |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Michael Adekunle | Executive Sponsor (Managing Partner) | Business outcome and timeline | High | High | Bi-weekly steering; dashboard previews |
| Grace Nnamdi | Product / Business Owner (Head of PMO) | Scope and user value | High | High | Weekly; owns RAG rules and template |
| Femi Balogun | Technology / Delivery Lead (BI Lead) | Feasibility and team capacity | Medium | High | Sprint planning; owns data model |

| IT Security | Access control | Who sees what | Medium | Low | Row-level security review |

## Power / interest summary
- **Manage closely (high influence, high interest):** Michael Adekunle, Grace Nnamdi
- **Keep satisfied (high influence, lower interest):** Practice Leads (6), Head of Finance
- **Keep informed (lower influence, high interest):** Femi Balogun, Halima Garba, Project Managers (35)
- **Monitor:** IT Security

## RACI matrix
R = Responsible · A = Accountable · C = Consulted · I = Informed

| Activity / deliverable | Sponsor | PM (Sola) | Head of PMO | BI Lead | Delivery Ops | Finance | Practice Leads |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Charter | A | R | C | C | I | I | I |
| Current/future-state process | I | A | R | C | R | I | C |
| RAG rules & data dictionary | I | R | A | C | C | C | C |
| Data model & finance feed | I | C | C | A | I | R | I |
| Dashboard build (sprints) | I | C | C | A | I | I | C |
| Training & adoption | I | C | C | I | A | I | I |
| Change requests | A | R | C | C | I | I | C |
| Benefits tracking | I | A | R | C | C | I | I |

**Rule applied:** exactly one **A** per row, so every deliverable has a single accountable owner.
