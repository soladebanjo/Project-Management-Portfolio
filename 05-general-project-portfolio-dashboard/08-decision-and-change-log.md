## Event: a senior stakeholder asks for a late change that sounds small
**What happened (Week 12):** A practice lead asked to "just add a page showing utilisation for each consultant."

**How I assessed it**
| Impact area | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Schedule | Needs a new timesheet-system feed: +3 weeks, which pushes go-live past annual planning (fixed date) |
| Cost | +$22K (data engineering and BI time), more than remaining contingency ($14K) |
| Risk | Individual performance data raises privacy and HR policy questions and needs HR consultation |
| Value | Real need, but practice-level utilisation answers 80% of the question |

**Options:** (A) add it now: miss the fixed date; (B) **defer to Phase 2 and add practice-level utilisation now from the existing resourcing export (no new feed, fits Sprint 4)**; (C) reject. I recommended **B**, and the practice lead agreed once they saw the impact on the annual-planning date.
