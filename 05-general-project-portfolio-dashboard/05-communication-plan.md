# Communication and Status-Reporting Plan – Project Portfolio Dashboard

> **Simulated project.** Organisation, people and data are fictional and based on a PM Project Lab practice brief. The PM artefacts are my own work, produced to practise real project-management delivery.

| Audience | Information | Format / channel | Frequency | Owner | Escalation route |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Steering group | Progress, decisions, risks | Meeting + 1-page status | Every 2 weeks | PM | Sponsor |
| Practice champions | Working dashboard, feedback | Sprint review demo | Every 2 weeks | BI Lead | PM |
| Project managers | New template, deadlines, how-to | 30-min training, Teams channel, quick guide | Weekly during rollout | Delivery Ops | Head of PMO |
| Practice leads | Practice view, RAG rules | Monthly demo | Monthly | Head of PMO | Sponsor |
| All staff | Why we're changing | Managing Partner note | Once at launch | Sponsor | — |

## Escalation path
Team → PM (24 h) → Head of PMO for scope/RAG questions → Sponsor / steering group for date, budget or cross-practice disputes.

## Status reporting rules
- RAG status is evidence-based: **Green** = on plan; **Amber** = variance within tolerance with a recovery plan; **Red** = outside tolerance, sponsor decision needed.
- Tolerances: go-live date fixed (Week 16, zero tolerance), budget ±5%, scope flexible through backlog.
- Every report states what changed since last week, decisions needed, and top risks with owners.
