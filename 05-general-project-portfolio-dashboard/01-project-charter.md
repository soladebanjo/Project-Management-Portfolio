# Project Charter – Project Portfolio Dashboard

> **Simulated project.** Organisation, people and data are fictional and based on a PM Project Lab practice brief. The PM artefacts are my own work, produced to practise real project-management delivery.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Organisation | Meridian Advisory (fictional) – professional services firm with 6 practices and ~40 active client projects |
| Industry | General / cross-industry (Professional Services & PMO) |
| Project Manager | Sola Debanjo, PMP |
| Executive Sponsor | Michael Adekunle, Managing Partner |
| Delivery approach | Hybrid (Waterfall for data & governance, Agile sprints for dashboard build) |
| Duration | 18 weeks (Week 1 = 05 Jan 2026, simulated) |
| Team size | 9 |
| Budget (assumed) | CAD $150,000 (assumed; includes 10% contingency) |

## 1. Business problem
Project status is reported inconsistently (different templates, RAG definitions and timing). Data is re-keyed by hand, leaders don't trust it, and PMs spend hours each week preparing reports instead of managing delivery.

## 2. Purpose
Introduce a standard weekly status template, RAG rules and a single portfolio dashboard (Power BI) that leaders trust and use to make decisions.

## 3. Objectives and success measures
| # | Objective | Measure / target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Standardise reporting | 100% of active projects report via the standard template by Week 14 |
| 2 | Cut reporting effort | PM weekly report preparation from ~3 h (simulated baseline) to ≤ 1 h |
| 3 | Trusted data | ≥ 95% of required fields complete and on time each week |
| 4 | Decision-ready view | Dashboard used as the agenda for the weekly portfolio meeting from Week 16 |
| 5 | Deliver on time | Live before annual planning (Week 16); closure Week 18; within budget |

*Baselines and targets are simulated planning assumptions, not real-world results.*

## 4. Scope
**In scope**
- Current-state review of reporting across 6 practices
- Standard status template (Microsoft Forms / SharePoint list)
- RAG rules and data dictionary
- Data model combining status, finance (budget vs actual) and resourcing data
- Power BI portfolio dashboard (3 pages)
- Training for 35 PMs and leaders; adoption tracking

**Out of scope**
- Consultant-level performance/utilisation reporting (Phase 2, see CR-02)
- Replacing finance or timesheet systems
- Client-facing reports

## 5. Key deliverables
- Project charter
- Current-state / future-state reporting process map
- Data dictionary & RAG rules
- Portfolio dashboard (Power BI)
- Implementation plan with owners & acceptance criteria
- Benefits / KPI dashboard
- Training materials
- Closure report

## 7. Assumptions
- Finance can provide a weekly budget-vs-actual export
- PMs spend 30 min in training
- Power BI licences already available
- Each practice nominates a reporting champion

## 8. Constraints
- Billable staff availability may affect the critical path (PMs and partners are on client work)
- Sponsor wants quick progress; BI lead protecting quality
- The business must keep operating; no reporting gap during the switch-over
- Limited contingency; scope changes through change control

## 9. High-level risks
- PMs see the template as extra admin and don't submit on time (owner: Delivery Ops)
- Practice leads dispute their RAG ratings (owner: Head of PMO)
- Finance data doesn't reconcile with project budgets (owner: BI Lead)
- Late scope requests from senior partners threaten fixed date (owner: PM)

## 11. Governance
**Steering group** every 2 weeks (Managing Partner – chair, Head of PMO, Head of Finance, PM). **Sprint reviews** every 2 weeks with practice champions. The PM and Head of PMO approve changes under $5K or 1 week; anything larger goes to the steering group.
