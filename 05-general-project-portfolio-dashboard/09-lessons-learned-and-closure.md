# Lessons Learned and Closure Summary – Project Portfolio Dashboard

> **Simulated project.** Organisation, people and data are fictional and based on a PM Project Lab practice brief. The PM artefacts are my own work, produced to practise real project-management delivery.

## Closure summary (Week 18 – simulated)
| Objective | Target | Simulated outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Standard template adoption | 100% by Week 14 | 100% (40/40) by Week 14 |
| PM report preparation time | ≤ 1 h | 50 min average (survey, 31 PMs) |
| Data complete & on time | ≥ 95% | 96% |
| Go-live before annual planning | Week 16 | Week 16 |
| Budget | $150K | $141K |

## Lessons learned
| What happened | Lesson / what I will do next time |
|---|---|
| RAG disputes stopped once rules were agreed up front | Agree definitions before building any dashboard; data standards come first |
| Pre-filling finance data cut PM effort | Reduce user effort before asking for compliance |
| Late "small" request would have missed the fixed date | Show impact in numbers (time, cost, risk); a Phase 2 backlog lets you say "not now" without saying "no" |
| Showing missing submissions on the dashboard improved compliance | Make adoption visible to leaders |
