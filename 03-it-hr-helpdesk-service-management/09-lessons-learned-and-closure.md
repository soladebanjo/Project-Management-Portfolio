# Lessons Learned and Closure Summary – HR Helpdesk & Case Management System

> **Simulated project.** Organisation, people and data are fictional and based on a PM Project Lab practice brief. The PM artefacts are my own work, produced to practise real project-management delivery.

| Launch | Week 13 | Week 13 |
| Budget | $180K | $171K |

## Lessons learned
| What happened | Lesson / what I will do next time |
|---|---|
| Employees did not recognise "Employee relations" as the confidential option | Test labels and request types with real employees before pilot (5-minute hallway tests) |
| Piloting first exposed the privacy gap with limited impact | Always pilot when sensitive data is involved |
| HRBPs' resistance eased once 'preferred adviser' routing was added | Address the real concern behind resistance (relationships), not just the process |
| Negative permission tests were missing from Done | Include "who must NOT see this" tests in Definition of Done from Sprint 1 |
