# Communication and Status-Reporting Plan – HR Helpdesk & Case Management System (IT Service Management)

> **Simulated project.** Organisation, people and data are fictional and based on a PM Project Lab practice brief. The PM artefacts are my own work, produced to practise real project-management delivery.

| Audience | Information | Format / channel | Frequency | Owner | Escalation route |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sponsor & checkpoint group | Progress, burn-up, risks, decisions | Monthly checkpoint + 1-page update | Monthly / weekly email | PM | Sponsor |
| Scrum team | Sprint goal, blockers | Daily stand-up (15 min), sprint planning, retro | Daily / bi-weekly | PM | Product Owner |
| Stakeholders | Working software | Sprint review demo | Every 2 weeks | Product Owner | PM |
| HR agents & HRBPs | Process changes, training, 'what's in it for me' | Team sessions, 1:1s, user guide | Weekly during pilot/launch | HR Ops Lead | PM |
| All employees | How to get HR help, privacy assurance | Intranet article, email from CHRO, posters | Pilot launch + bank-wide launch | Product Owner | Sponsor |
| Privacy & Compliance | Permission design, incidents | Review meeting; immediate notice for incidents | Per sprint / same day | PM | Sponsor |

## Escalation path
Blocker raised at stand-up → PM removes within 24 h or escalates to Product Owner (scope) / ITSM Lead (technical) → Sponsor for budget, date or policy decisions. **Any privacy incident goes to the Privacy Officer and Sponsor the same day.**

## Status reporting rules
- RAG status is evidence-based: **Green** = on plan; **Amber** = variance within tolerance with a recovery plan; **Red** = outside tolerance, sponsor decision needed.
- Tolerances: launch date ±1 week, budget ±5%, zero tolerance for unauthorised access to confidential cases.
- Every report states what changed since last week, decisions needed, and top risks with owners.
