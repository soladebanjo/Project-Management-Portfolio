# Stakeholder Register and RACI – HR Helpdesk & Case Management System (IT Service Management)

> **Simulated project.** Organisation, people and data are fictional and based on a PM Project Lab practice brief. The PM artefacts are my own work, produced to practise real project-management delivery.

## Stakeholder register
| Stakeholder | Role | Primary concern | Influence | Interest | Engagement approach |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| James Okoro | Executive Sponsor (CHRO) | Business outcome and timeline | High | High | Monthly checkpoint; sprint review demos |
| Joseph Mensah | Product Owner (HR Operations Manager) | Scope and user value | High | High | Daily availability; owns backlog priority |
| Kelechi Nwosu | Technology / Delivery Lead (IT Service Management Lead) | Feasibility and team capacity | High | High | Sprint planning; owns configuration & SSO |
| Mariam Bello | Operations Lead (HR Shared Services Supervisor) | Adoption and service continuity | Medium | High | Leads HR agent team; pilot coordinator |
| HR Business Partners | HR advisers (resistant group) | Losing personal relationship with 'their' employees | Medium | High | 1:1s; 'preferred adviser' field; involve in workflow design |
| Privacy & Compliance Officer | Compliance | Confidential case segregation, retention | High | Medium | Reviews permission design; sign-off before pilot |
| Information Security | IT Security | Access control, SSO | Medium | Low | Security review in Sprint 2 |
| Employees (pilot departments) | Service users | Easy to ask, quick answers, privacy | Low | High | Pilot feedback survey; launch comms |

## Power / interest summary
- **Manage closely (high influence, high interest):** James Okoro, Joseph Mensah, Kelechi Nwosu
- **Keep satisfied (high influence, lower interest):** Privacy & Compliance Officer
- **Keep informed (lower influence, high interest):** Mariam Bello, HR Business Partners, Employees (pilot departments)
- **Monitor:** Information Security

## RACI matrix
R = Responsible · A = Accountable · C = Consulted · I = Informed

| Activity / deliverable | Sponsor | PM (Sola) | Product Owner | ITSM Lead | HR Ops Lead | Privacy Officer | Config Partner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Charter & budget | A | R | C | C | I | I | I |
| Backlog prioritisation | I | C | A | C | C | I | I |
| Portal & queue configuration | I | C | C | A | C | I | R |
| Confidential workflow & permissions | I | R | C | R | C | A | R |
| Knowledge base content | I | C | A | I | R | I | I |
| HR staff training & change management | I | A | C | I | R | I | C |
| UAT sign-off | I | R | A | R | R | C | C |
| Launch go / no-go | A | R | C | C | C | C | I |
| Hypercare & handover to IT support | I | A | C | R | R | I | C |

**Rule applied:** exactly one **A** per row, so every deliverable has a single accountable owner.
