## Lessons learned
| What happened | Impact | Lesson / what I will do next time |
|---|---|---|
| Vendor reported progress as "% complete" | Real progress hidden until milestone was missed | Define milestone evidence (e.g., test cases passed) in the plan and contract from day 1 |
| Interface team capacity dropped during holidays | 2-week slip on the critical path | Get external dependency commitments in writing **with** holiday cover, and review them weekly in the RAID log |
| Reminder wording was agreed with the Privacy Officer early | Avoided late re-work | Bring compliance in at requirements stage, not at testing |
| Super-users from each clinic | Fast adoption, fewer tickets | Repeat the super-user model on every system rollout |
| Holding 1 week of contingency before go/no-go | Absorbed the vendor slip without cost | Always protect contingency close to the critical milestone |
