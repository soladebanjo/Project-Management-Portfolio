# Communication and Status-Reporting Plan – Clinic Appointment Scheduling Upgrade

> **Simulated project.** Organisation, people and data are fictional and based on a PM Project Lab practice brief. The PM artefacts are my own work, produced to practise real project-management delivery.

| Audience | Information | Format / channel | Frequency | Owner | Escalation route |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Steering Committee | Status, RAG, budget, decisions needed | Steering meeting + 1-page report | Monthly | PM | Sponsor |
| Executive Sponsor | Progress, top risks, decisions | 1-page email update + 15-min call | Weekly | PM | Steering Committee |
| Project working group | Plan, RAID, actions | Teams meeting + RAID log | Weekly | PM | Sponsor |
| Vendor | Milestone evidence, defects | Checkpoint call + defect tracker | Weekly | IT Lead | PM → Sponsor |
| Clinic managers & physicians | What changes, when, what they need to do | Clinic huddle slot + newsletter | Bi-weekly | Clinical Lead | PM |
| Booking & reception staff | Training dates, go-live support | Team briefings, quick-reference guides | Bi-weekly, daily in go-live week | Booking Lead | PM |
| Patients | New reminder messages | Clinic posters, website notice | Once before go-live | Clinical Lead | — |

## Escalation path
Team member → workstream lead (24 h) → PM (48 h) → Sponsor (next weekly call, or same day if patient safety is affected) → Steering Committee.

## Status reporting rules
- RAG status is evidence-based: **Green** = on plan; **Amber** = variance within tolerance with a recovery plan; **Red** = outside tolerance, sponsor decision needed.
- Tolerances: schedule ±1 week, budget ±5%, zero tolerance on patient-safety defects.
- Every report states what changed since last week, decisions needed, and top risks with owners.
