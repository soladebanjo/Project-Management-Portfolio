# Prioritised Scope Backlog – Clinic Appointment Scheduling Upgrade

> **Simulated project.** Organisation, people and data are fictional and based on a PM Project Lab practice brief. The PM artefacts are my own work, produced to practise real project-management delivery.

Prioritised with **MoSCoW**. "Must" items define the minimum viable go-live; "Should" items go into Release 1.1 (4 weeks after go-live) if not ready. Full table: [`scope-backlog.csv`](scope-backlog.csv).

**Release boundaries**
- **Go-live (Week 18):** B-01 to B-06 (all Must items) + B-03.
- **Release 1.1 (Week 22, after closure, owned by IT Applications):** B-07, B-09.
- **Phase 2 (separate business case):** online self-booking, multi-language reminders.

Any new item raised after Week 6 goes through change control: the PM assesses impact on schedule, cost and risk before it can be added to the go-live release.
