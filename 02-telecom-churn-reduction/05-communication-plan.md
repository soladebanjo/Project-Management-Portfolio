# Communication and Status-Reporting Plan – Customer Churn Reduction Project

> **Simulated project.** Organisation, people and data are fictional and based on a PM Project Lab practice brief. The PM artefacts are my own work, produced to practise real project-management delivery.

| Audience | Information | Format / channel | Frequency | Owner | Escalation route |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Steering Committee | Gate readiness, RAG, budget, decisions | Steering pack (5 slides) | Monthly + each gate | PM | Sponsor |
| Executive Sponsor | Status, risks, decisions | 1-page status report | Weekly | PM | Steering Committee |
| Workstream leads | Plan, RAID, dependencies | Delivery meeting + RAID log | Weekly | PM | Sponsor |
| Contact centre agents | Save-desk changes, scripts, training | Team huddles, intranet FAQ | Bi-weekly; daily at launch | Contact Centre Manager | PM |
| Field sales | SME retention process | Sales meeting slot | Monthly | Field Sales Lead | Business Owner |
| Finance | Offer spend vs saves | Benefits dashboard extract | Monthly after go-live | BI/CRM Lead | Sponsor |

## Escalation path
Workstream lead (2 days) → PM → Sponsor at weekly update (same day for anything that could affect live customer service) → Steering Committee for gate, budget or scope decisions.

## Status reporting rules
- RAG status is evidence-based: **Green** = on plan; **Amber** = variance within tolerance with a recovery plan; **Red** = outside tolerance, sponsor decision needed.
- Tolerances: schedule ±2 weeks, budget ±5%; any risk to live customer service is escalated the same day.
- Every report states what changed since last week, decisions needed, and top risks with owners.
