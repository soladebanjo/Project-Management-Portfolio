# Stakeholder Register and RACI – Customer Churn Reduction Project

> **Simulated project.** Organisation, people and data are fictional and based on a PM Project Lab practice brief. The PM artefacts are my own work, produced to practise real project-management delivery.

| Stakeholder | Role | Primary concern | Influence | Interest | Engagement approach |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daniel Adebayo | Executive Sponsor (CCO) | Business outcome and timeline | High | High | Monthly steering; gate approvals; weekly update |

## Power / interest summary
- **Manage closely (high influence, high interest):** Daniel Adebayo, Ngozi Ibe, Emeka Umeh, Ruth Asante
- **Keep satisfied (high influence, lower interest):** Finance Business Partner, Legal & Privacy
- **Keep informed (lower influence, high interest):** Field Sales Lead, Contact centre agents
- **Monitor:** Network Operations Manager

## RACI matrix
R = Responsible · A = Accountable · C = Consulted · I = Informed

| Activity / deliverable | Sponsor | PM (Sola) | Business Owner | BI/CRM Lead | Contact Centre | Finance | Legal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Charter & gate approvals | A | R | C | C | C | I | I |
| Current/future-state process map | I | A | R | C | R | I | I |
| Churn-risk scoring rules | I | C | A | R | C | I | C |
| Data mart & risk report build | I | C | C | A | I | I | I |
| Offer matrix & approval workflow | C | R | A | R | C | C | I |
| Save-desk set-up & training | I | C | C | I | A | I | I |
| Win-back campaign consent review | I | R | C | I | I | I | A |
| UAT sign-off | I | R | A | R | R | I | I |
| Go / no-go | A | R | C | C | C | I | C |
| Benefits / KPI dashboard | I | A | C | R | C | C | I |

**Rule applied:** exactly one **A** per row, so every deliverable has a single accountable owner.
