# Customer Churn Reduction Project

**Telecommunications · Waterfall (stage-gated) · 43 weeks · Team of 17 · Budget CAD $1.2M (assumed)**

> **Simulated project.** Organisation, people and data are fictional and based on a PM Project Lab practice brief. The PM artefacts are my own work, produced to practise real project-management delivery.

## The situation
ClearWave is losing home-internet and small-business customers to fibre and satellite competitors. Nobody can see which customers are at risk until they call to cancel, retention offers are approved case by case, and complaints take too long to resolve. Leadership wants a controlled programme to cut churn **while the business keeps running**: no service interruption, and the sales and contact centre teams keep working throughout.

## My role
I acted as **Project Manager**: I ran the stage-gated plan across data/BI, CRM, the contact centre, field sales and finance; owned the charter, schedule, RAID log and status reporting; mapped the current and future retention process with the business; set up the benefits/KPI dashboard; and led the go-live decision when UAT found a serious defect two weeks before launch.

## Key PM decision
Two weeks before go-live, UAT found that duplicate SME account IDs were giving some customers the wrong churn-risk score. I classified it as critical, contained it the same day and gave the steering committee three costed options. Then I recommended a **phased go-live**: residential customers on time and SME customers 3 weeks later. That protected most of the benefit without risking contact with the wrong customer.
