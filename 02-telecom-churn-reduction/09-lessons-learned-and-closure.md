# Lessons Learned and Closure Summary – Customer Churn Reduction Project

> **Simulated project.** Organisation, people and data are fictional and based on a PM Project Lab practice brief. The PM artefacts are my own work, produced to practise real project-management delivery.

## Closure summary (Week 43 – simulated)
| Objective | Target | Simulated outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Go-live | Week 37 | Residential Week 37; SME Week 40 (CR-05) |
| Budget | $1.2M ±5% | $1.17M final |

| Customer-facing outages caused by project | 0 | 0 |

## Lessons learned
| What happened | Lesson / what I will do next time |
|---|---|
| Duplicate account IDs found in UAT, not earlier | Run data-quality profiling on **every** customer segment during requirements, not just the largest one |
| Stakeholders asked for SME alerts after seeing the first report | Show a prototype report at the requirements gate; expect change and budget for it |
| Phased go-live protected 85% of the benefit | Design releases so segments can go live independently |
| Agent focus groups shaped the scripts | Involve frontline users before design sign-off, not at training |
| Benefits baseline agreed with Finance at Gate 3 | Lock baselines early so benefits cannot be disputed later |
