# Project Charter – Construction Cost Control Recovery

> **Simulated project.** Organisation, people and data are fictional and based on a PM Project Lab practice brief. The PM artefacts are my own work, produced to practise real project-management delivery.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Organisation | Lakeline Developments (fictional) – mid-sized property company; 6-storey, 96-unit residential building in Edmonton, AB |
| Industry | Construction & Real Estate |
| Project Manager | Sola Debanjo, PMP |
| Executive Sponsor | Fatima Sule, Development Director |
| Delivery approach | Hybrid (Waterfall construction schedule + 2-week Agile sprints to build cost controls) |
| Duration | 51 weeks (Week 1 = 11 May 2026, simulated) |
| Team size | 14 |
| Budget (assumed) | CAD $650,000 for the recovery programme (assumed); controls a $38M construction budget |

## 1. Business problem
Cost is not controlled: there are verbal change orders, no committed-cost register, and invoices are paid on percentage claims that nobody verifies on site. Forecast final cost is $41.4M (+9%), which threatens the lender's covenant and the project's margin.

## 2. Purpose
Recover cost control and complete construction within +3% of the original $38M budget, while keeping quality and safety, through clear governance, verified progress payments and earned-value reporting.

## 3. Objectives and success measures
| # | Objective | Measure / target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bring final cost back under control | Forecast final cost ≤ $39.14M (≤ +3% of $38M), down from $41.4M (+9%) |
| 2 | Control change | 100% of change orders written, priced and approved before work starts; decision within 10 business days |
| 3 | Verify every payment | 100% of progress invoices checked against site-verified % complete before payment |
| 4 | Transparent reporting | Monthly EVM report (CPI, SPI, EAC) to sponsor and lender from Month 2 |
| 5 | Hold cost efficiency | Cost Performance Index (CPI) ≥ 0.97 by Month 6 |
| 6 | Hand over safely | Occupancy permit and handover by Week 51 with zero open safety-critical defects |

*Baselines and targets are simulated planning assumptions, not real-world results.*

## 4. Scope
**In scope**
- Cost audit and re-baseline of remaining scope (cost-to-complete $22.8M)
- Committed-cost register for all trade packages
- Change-order process with approval limits
- Invoice-to-progress verification on site
- Monthly EVM and lender reporting
- Procurement / contractor tracker and quality inspection plan
- Coordination of remaining construction to handover
- Handover, snagging and acceptance

**Out of scope**
- Design changes to unit layouts
- Sales and marketing of units
- Disputes already in legal process (handled by legal counsel)
- Property management after handover

## 5. Key deliverables
- Recovery charter and re-baselined budget
- WBS and site execution plan
- Committed-cost register & change-order log
- Procurement / contractor tracker and quality inspection plan
- Monthly EVM cost report
- Handover, snagging and acceptance checklist
- Closure report

## 7. Assumptions
- General contractor agrees to the new invoice-verification process (added to contract by amendment)
- Lender accepts monthly EVM report as its monitoring report
- Cost consultant (quantity surveyor) available 3 days/week
- No major design changes after re-baseline

## 8. Constraints
- Weather and site access affect the critical path; Edmonton winter makes envelope close-in before freeze-up critical
- Dependencies outside the core team (city inspections, utility connections) can move the critical path
- Approval delays could use up schedule contingency
- Limited contingency: any scope increase goes through formal change control

## 9. High-level risks
- Window supplier misses delivery, so the envelope is not closed before winter (owner: Commercial Mgr)
- Contractor resists invoice verification, slowing payments and goodwill (owner: PM)
- Hidden unapproved change orders appear in final accounts (owner: Commercial Mgr)
- City inspection booking delays (owner: Construction Mgr)
- Material price increase on remaining packages (owner: Commercial Mgr)

## 11. Governance
**Project Control Board** every 2 weeks (Development Director – chair, Commercial Manager, Construction Manager, PM, cost consultant). Change orders: up to $25K – PM + Commercial Manager; $25K–$100K – Development Director; over $100K – Control Board and lender notified. Monthly report to the lender's monitor.
