# WBS and Site Execution Plan – Construction Cost Control Recovery

> **Simulated project.** Organisation, people and data are fictional and based on a PM Project Lab practice brief. The PM artefacts are my own work, produced to practise real project-management delivery.

| Work package | Sequence / dependency | Owner | Inspection / hold point |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structure floors 3–6 & roof | Floor by floor; roof before envelope top floor | General Contractor | Framing inspection per floor (city) |
| Windows & cladding | Starts floor 1 once framing passes; must close in before freeze-up (Week 24) | General Contractor / ClearSpan | Water test on sample window per floor |
| Roofing | After structure complete | Summit Roof Systems | Roof membrane inspection |
| MEP rough-in | Floor by floor after close-in (or temporary enclosure) | Prairie Mechanical / Bright Circuit | Pre-drywall inspection (city) – hold point |
| Fire stopping & life safety | Before drywall closes each floor | General Contractor | Independent fire-stopping review – hold point |
| Interiors & finishes | After pre-drywall inspection per floor | Capital Interiors / River Valley | Unit-by-unit snag list |
| Elevators | Shaft ready Week 36; install 9 weeks | Vertex Lifts | Elevator safety inspection |
| Commissioning | After permanent power (D-02) | Construction Mgr | Fire alarm, sprinkler, HVAC tests |

**Weekly look-ahead:** the general contractor issues a 3-week look-ahead every Monday; the PM checks it against the baseline and flags any critical-path slip at the Wednesday progress meeting.

**Safety:** the contractor's safety plan applies; any safety stop-work is escalated to the Sponsor the same day.
