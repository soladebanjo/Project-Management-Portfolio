# Stakeholder Register and RACI – Construction Cost Control Recovery

> **Simulated project.** Organisation, people and data are fictional and based on a PM Project Lab practice brief. The PM artefacts are my own work, produced to practise real project-management delivery.

## Stakeholder register
| Stakeholder | Role | Primary concern | Influence | Interest | Engagement approach |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fatima Sule | Project Sponsor (Development Director) | Schedule, cost and business outcome | High | High | Bi-weekly control board; monthly EVM report; decision memos |
| Michael Adekunle | Technical / Site Lead (Construction Manager) | Feasibility, sequencing and quality | High | High | Daily site check-ins; weekly look-ahead; owns site verification |
| Grace Nnamdi | Commercial / Procurement Lead (Commercial Manager) | Supplier performance and cost control | High | High | Weekly cost meeting; owns commitments and change orders |
| Femi Balogun | Operations / End User Lead (Property Operations Manager) | Continuity, safety and acceptance | Medium | Medium | Monthly; leads handover acceptance and O&M manuals |
| General Contractor PM | Main contractor | Payment, schedule, change approvals | High | High | Weekly progress meeting; invoice verification walk |
| Cost consultant (QS) | Advisor | Accurate valuation | Medium | High | Weekly; values progress and change orders |
| Lender's monitor | Financier | Budget covenant, drawdowns | High | Medium | Monthly EVM report; site visit quarterly |
| City building inspector | Regulator | Code compliance, permits | High | Low | Inspection booking 2 weeks ahead; pre-inspections |
| Window / curtain-wall supplier | Key supplier | Production schedule, payment | Medium | Medium | Bi-weekly production updates; factory evidence |

## Power / interest summary
- **Manage closely (high influence, high interest):** Fatima Sule, Michael Adekunle, Grace Nnamdi, General Contractor PM
- **Keep satisfied (high influence, lower interest):** Lender's monitor, City building inspector
- **Keep informed (lower influence, high interest):** Cost consultant (QS)
- **Monitor:** Femi Balogun, Window / curtain-wall supplier

## RACI matrix
R = Responsible · A = Accountable · C = Consulted · I = Informed

| Activity / deliverable | Sponsor | PM (Sola) | Construction Mgr | Commercial Mgr | General Contractor | Cost Consultant | Ops Lead |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recovery charter & re-baseline | A | R | C | C | C | R | I |
| Committed-cost register | I | A | C | R | C | C | I |
| Change-order assessment & approval | A | R | C | R | C | R | I |
| Invoice-to-progress verification | I | A | R | C | C | R | I |
| Monthly EVM & lender report | I | A | C | C | I | R | I |
| Site execution & sequencing | I | C | A | I | R | I | I |
| Quality inspections | I | C | A | I | R | I | C |
| Handover & snagging acceptance | A | R | R | I | R | I | R |

**Rule applied:** exactly one **A** per row, so every deliverable has a single accountable owner.
