## How invoices are verified before payment
1. Contractor submits a progress claim with % complete per package.
2. Weekly joint **verification walk** (Construction Manager + cost consultant + contractor).
3. Cost consultant values **site-verified** % complete, not the claimed %.
4. PM checks against the committed-cost register and approved change orders.
5. Commercial Manager approves; payment within 15 days.

**Week 20 example (simulated):** the mechanical claim was 35% complete; site-verified was 29%. Payment was reduced by $306,000 until the work was in place.

## Quality inspection plan
| Hold point | Inspector | Acceptance criteria | Record |
|---|---|---|---|
| Framing (each floor) | City inspector | Passed inspection report | Inspection log |
| Window water test (sample per floor) | Independent tester | No leakage at test pressure per spec | Test report + photos |
| Fire stopping (each floor) | Independent reviewer | All penetrations sealed per approved system | Review report |
| Pre-drywall (each floor) | City inspector | MEP rough-in passed | Inspection log |
| Elevator | Provincial elevator inspector | Certificate issued | Certificate |
| Life safety systems | Commissioning agent | Fire alarm & sprinkler tests passed | Commissioning report |
| Occupancy | City | Occupancy permit issued | Permit |
