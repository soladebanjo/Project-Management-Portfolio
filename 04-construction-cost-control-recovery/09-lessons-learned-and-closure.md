## Lessons learned
| What happened | Lesson / what I will do next time |
|---|---|
| $1.1M of verbal change orders found in audit | Set change-order rules and approval limits on day 1 of any project; no paperwork, no payment |
| Claimed % complete was often higher than site-verified | Pay on verified progress only; a fixed weekly verification walk builds trust and routine |
| Supplier said "2 weeks late" but its factory data showed 5 | Ask for production evidence, not opinions; track long-lead items bi-weekly from order |
| Winter close-in was the real critical path | Map seasonal constraints into the baseline schedule, especially in a climate like Edmonton’s |
| EVM gave the lender confidence | Agree the reporting format with financiers early so one report serves everyone |
