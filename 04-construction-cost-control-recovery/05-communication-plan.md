# Communication and Status-Reporting Plan – Construction Cost Control Recovery

> **Simulated project.** Organisation, people and data are fictional and based on a PM Project Lab practice brief. The PM artefacts are my own work, produced to practise real project-management delivery.

| Audience | Information | Format / channel | Frequency | Owner | Escalation route |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Project Control Board | Cost, EVM, change orders, decisions | Meeting + control pack | Every 2 weeks | PM | Sponsor |
| Sponsor | Status, top risks, decisions | 1-page update | Weekly | PM | Control Board |
| Lender's monitor | EVM, forecast final cost, schedule | Monthly EVM report | Monthly | PM | Sponsor |
| General contractor | Progress, look-ahead, invoice verification | Site progress meeting + verification walk | Weekly | Construction Mgr | PM |
| Suppliers | Production and delivery status | Supplier call + evidence (photos, shipping docs) | Bi-weekly | Commercial Mgr | PM |
| Property operations | Handover readiness, O&M, training | Handover meeting | Monthly; weekly from Week 44 | PM | Sponsor |

## Escalation path
Site issue → Construction Manager (same day) → PM (24 h) → Sponsor (48 h, or same day if safety or cost over $100K) → Project Control Board / lender notification.

## Status reporting rules
- RAG status is evidence-based: **Green** = on plan; **Amber** = variance within tolerance with a recovery plan; **Red** = outside tolerance, sponsor decision needed.
- Tolerances: cost: forecast final cost within +3% of $38M; schedule ±2 weeks; zero tolerance on safety.
- Every report states what changed since last week, decisions needed, and top risks with owners.
